# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Northwind Labs builds software products and runs customer-support operations from offices in Noida, Gurugram, Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad, with remote teams across India. This handbook covers the policies that apply to all full-time employees and interns. Policy owner: the People (HR) team. Last revised: November 2026.

## Working hours

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Teams may agree on flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM as long as core collaboration hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM are covered.

Customer-support (BPO) teams work in rotational shifts of 9 hours including breaks, five days a week, with two weekly offs that may fall on weekdays. Night-shift employees (shifts starting after 8:00 PM) receive a night-shift allowance of INR 3,000 per month and company cab drop.

Attendance is marked through the HR portal. Three late check-ins (after the flexible start window) in a month count as half a day of annual leave.

## Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that lapses on 31 December. Annual leave is not encashed except at exit, when up to 10 days of balance are paid out in the full and final settlement.

Employees also get 8 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave and paternity leave is 10 working days, both available after 80 days of service. Adoption leave is 12 weeks for the primary caregiver. Bereavement leave is 5 working days for the loss of an immediate family member. Marriage leave is 3 working days, once during employment.

Leave requests must be raised in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies. The reporting manager approves leave; HR only steps in for disputes. Interns get 1 day of paid leave per month of internship and no carry-forward.

## Public holidays

There are 10 public holidays per year. The 2026 list: New Year's Day (1 January), Republic Day (26 January), Holi (4 March), Good Friday (3 April), Labour Day (1 May), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October), Dussehra (20 October), Diwali (8 November) and Christmas (25 December). Employees can also take 2 optional (restricted) holidays per year from the regional list published by HR. The list for the next year is published every December.

Employees who work on a public holiday because of a business need get a compensatory off, to be taken within 30 days.

## Work from home

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week after completing probation. Remote work beyond 2 days a week needs written approval from the department head. Interns can work from home 1 day per week with their mentor's approval. Roles advertised as "Remote" are fully remote and follow the same working hours.

A one-time home office allowance of INR 10,000 is reimbursed against bills for employees who have completed probation. Remote employees also receive an internet allowance of INR 1,000 per month.

## Probation

New full-time employees serve a probation period of 6 months. The reporting manager completes a probation review in the 5th month. Probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months, if the review is not satisfactory. During probation the notice period is 15 days.

## Notice period

After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for all roles at Senior level and above and 30 days for everyone else. Senior level and above means roles that need 5 or more years of experience (for example Senior, Lead, Manager and Director roles). Unused annual leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period. Buy-out of the notice period is allowed with approval from the department head and HR.

## Resignation and exit

Resignations are submitted in the HR portal and accepted by the reporting manager within 2 working days. On the last working day the employee returns the laptop, ID card and any other company assets. The full and final settlement, including salary for the days worked, leave encashment and reimbursements, is paid within 45 days of the last working day. The relieving letter and experience letter are issued along with the settlement.

## Payroll and payslips

Salaries are paid monthly and credited to the employee's bank account on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available in the HR portal on the same day. Attendance and leave are counted from the 26th of the previous month to the 25th of the current month for payroll.

Income tax is deducted at source. Employees declare their tax-saving investments in the HR portal in April and submit proofs by 15 January. Form 16 is issued by 15 June every year.

## Reimbursements and travel

Business expenses are claimed in the HR portal with bills within 30 days of spending and are paid with the next month's salary. Mobile phone bills are reimbursed up to INR 1,000 per month for roles that need it.

Domestic business travel is booked through the travel desk in the HR portal. Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 5,000 per night in metro cities and INR 3,500 per night elsewhere, and a daily allowance of INR 1,500 covers meals and local travel.

## Health insurance and benefits

Every full-time employee is covered from the date of joining by a group health insurance policy with a sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per year on a family-floater basis, covering the employee, spouse and up to two children. Parents or parents-in-law can be added at the employee's cost. Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one, and cashless treatment is available at network hospitals.

Employees also have term life insurance of three times their annual fixed salary and personal accident cover of INR 10,00,000. A free annual health check-up is available to employees over 30. The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling sessions.

Other benefits: a meal card of INR 2,200 per month, festival gift vouchers at Diwali, and company-paid cab drop for shifts ending after 9:00 PM.

## Learning and development

Each full-time employee has a learning budget of INR 30,000 per financial year for courses, certifications and conference tickets, approved by the reporting manager. Certification exam fees are reimbursed after the employee passes the exam. Employees who use more than INR 1,00,000 of sponsored training in a year sign a 12-month service agreement for that training.

Every employee gets access to the company's online learning library from the first day, and new joiners complete a 5-day induction programme in their first two weeks.

## Performance reviews and appraisals

Performance is reviewed in an annual cycle that runs from April to March, with a mid-year check-in in October. Goals are set in April with the reporting manager. Annual increments and promotions are announced in June and take effect from 1 April. Employees who joined after 1 January are reviewed in the next cycle.

Ratings use a five-point scale. Employees rated in the top two levels are eligible for the annual performance bonus, paid with the June salary.

## Employee referral bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus: INR 25,000 for roles needing up to 3 years of experience and INR 50,000 for roles needing more than 3 years. The bonus is paid in two equal parts: 50% after the new hire joins and 50% after they complete probation. Interns, HR team members and the hiring manager of the role are not eligible for a referral bonus for that role.

Referrals are submitted in the HR portal with the candidate's resume. A referral is valid for 6 months from submission.

## Interview process

Most roles follow four stages: an application screening by the HR assistant, a 30-minute HR call, one or two technical or functional interviews with the hiring team, and a final interview with the department head. Candidates usually hear back within 5 working days after each stage. Offers are rolled out within 3 working days of the final interview.

For internships the process is shorter: screening, one technical interview and an HR call. Customer-support roles have a voice and communication assessment in place of the technical interview.

Candidates who are not selected can apply again for the same role after 6 months.

## Offers and joining

An offer stays open for 7 days from the date it is sent. Background verification of education and previous employment starts after the offer is accepted. Most joiners start within 30 days of accepting, or after serving their notice period with the current employer.

## Documents required for joining

New joiners must submit: a government photo ID, PAN card, address proof, educational certificates and mark sheets, relieving letters from previous employers, the last three salary slips, and two passport-size photographs. Documents are uploaded to the HR portal before the joining date.

## Internal job postings

Employees who have spent at least 12 months in their current role can apply for internal openings. Inform your reporting manager before applying. Internal candidates go through the same interview stages, and the transfer usually takes effect within 30 days of selection.

## Code of conduct and dress code

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and candidates with respect, protect confidential company and customer information, and declare any conflict of interest to HR. The dress code is business casual from Monday to Thursday and casual on Fridays; customer-facing teams follow the client's dress guidelines on client visits.

Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Complaints of sexual harassment go to the Internal Committee, which can be reached confidentially through the HR portal and completes an inquiry within 90 days.

## IT and equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop on the first day. Personal devices may be used for company email only after enrolling them in the company's device-management app. Passwords are changed every 90 days, and lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

## Contact

For anything not covered here, contact the HR team through the HR portal ticketing system. Tickets are answered within 2 working days. Urgent payroll issues raised in the last week of the month are answered within 1 working day.
