# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (sample)

This is a fictional sample handbook for demonstrating the HR assistant. Northwind Labs is not a real company and none of these policies apply to any real organisation.

## Working hours

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Teams may agree on flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM as long as core collaboration hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM are covered.

## Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that lapses on 31 December.

Employees also get 8 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days needs a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal.

There are 10 public holidays per year; the list is published by HR every December. Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave and paternity leave is 10 working days, both available after 80 days of service.

Leave requests must be raised in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies. The reporting manager approves leave; HR only steps in for disputes.

## Work from home

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week after completing probation. Remote work beyond 2 days a week needs written approval from the department head. Interns can work from home 1 day per week with their mentor's approval.

A one-time home office allowance of INR 10,000 is reimbursed against bills for employees who have completed probation.

## Probation

New full-time employees serve a probation period of 6 months. The reporting manager completes a probation review in the 5th month. Probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months, if the review is not satisfactory. During probation the notice period is 15 days.

## Notice period

After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for all roles at Senior level and above and 30 days for everyone else. Unused annual leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period. Buy-out of the notice period is allowed with approval from the department head and HR.

## Employee referral bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus: INR 25,000 for roles needing up to 3 years of experience and INR 50,000 for roles needing more than 3 years. The bonus is paid in two equal parts: 50% after the new hire joins and 50% after they complete probation. Interns, HR team members and the hiring manager of the role are not eligible for a referral bonus for that role.

## Interview process

Most roles follow four stages: an application screening by the HR assistant, a 30-minute HR call, one or two technical or functional interviews with the hiring team, and a final interview with the department head. Candidates usually hear back within 5 working days after each stage. Offers are rolled out within 3 working days of the final interview.

For internships the process is shorter: screening, one technical interview and an HR call.

## Documents required for joining

New joiners must submit: a government photo ID, PAN card, address proof, educational certificates and mark sheets, relieving letters from previous employers, the last three salary slips, and two passport-size photographs. Documents are uploaded to the HR portal before the joining date.

## Internal job postings

Employees who have spent at least 12 months in their current role can apply for internal openings. Inform your reporting manager before applying. Internal candidates go through the same interview stages, and the transfer usually takes effect within 30 days of selection.

## Contact

For anything not covered here, contact the HR team through the HR portal ticketing system. Tickets are answered within 2 working days.
